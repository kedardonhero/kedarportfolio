1. Contact form

The contact section of the home page has a form with four fields: your name, your email address, a subject and a message. When you submit it, that information is sent to me through Formspree, a form-handling service, and delivered to my email inbox.

  • Why I collect it: only to read your message and reply to you.
  • What I do not do: I do not sell your information, add you to a mailing list or use it for marketing.
  • Formspree: as the processor of the form, Formspree may also receive technical details such as your IP address and browser type. How it handles this is described in Formspree's privacy policy.

Please do not include sensitive information, such as passwords or financial details, in a message.

2. Information collected automatically

This is a static website. It does not have user accounts and does not run a database of visitors. However, the service that hosts the site (currently GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, date and time, and the pages requested, in order to operate and secure the service. I do not have access to a personal profile of you from these logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

This site does not currently use an analytics tool. If I add one in the future, I will update this page.

3. Cookies and local storage

Cookies set by this site: none. I do not set my own cookies.

Local storage: your browser's local storage is used for features that stay on your device and are never sent to me:

  • Your theme choice (light, dark or system) and chosen accent colour, so the site looks the way you left it.
  • The Smart Trash Bin demo generates its bin data in your browser and does not store or send it anywhere. Other demo apps in my portfolio, such as the Personal Expense Tracker and the Shift Time Tracker, save the entries you make in your own browser so they are still there when you return. This data never leaves your device through this site. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser settings.

Third-party services, and advertising partners if ads are shown (see section 5), can set their own cookies. Those are covered below.

4. Third-party services

To display the site, your browser loads some resources from other providers. When it does, that provider receives your IP address and basic browser information, as with any website request. I do not control how they handle it.

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) – typefaces. Google Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) – Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons.
  • unpkg (unpkg.com) – the typing-animation library and the Leaflet map library used by the Smart Trash Bin demo.
  • OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) – map tiles in the Smart Trash Bin demo. The tile servers receive your IP address and the map area you view. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • Formspree – the contact form, as described in section 1.

The site also links to social profiles (Facebook, Instagram, X and LinkedIn). Those sites have their own privacy policies and only receive information if you click through to them.

5. Advertising

Current status: this site does not currently display advertisements. The rest of this section describes how advertising works if I enable Google AdSense, so that it is clear in advance. I will update this page before ads go live.

If ads are shown, they will be served by Google as a third-party vendor. In that case:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies, such as the DoubleClick cookie, enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • You can read more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, I will show a consent notice and only load personalised advertising if you agree.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Ads may then be less relevant to you, but the site will still work.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask me at any time to tell you what information of yours I hold (for example, a message you sent through the contact form), to correct it, or to delete it. Email me at eyeamkedar@gmail.com and I will respond as soon as I reasonably can. Depending on where you live, such as the European Economic Area or California, you may have additional legal rights over your personal information. I do not sell personal information.

7. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent me personal information, contact me and I will delete it.

8. Security and retention

The site is served over HTTPS where the host supports it, and the contact form sends data to Formspree over an encrypted connection. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security. I keep contact messages only as long as needed to respond and to keep a reasonable record of professional correspondence, and I delete them on request.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy, for example when I add analytics or advertising. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to eyeamkedar@gmail.com, or through the contact form.
